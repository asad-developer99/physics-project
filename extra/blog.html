Pascal's Law Explained: How a Small Push Lifts a Car

Squeeze a sealed water balloon and the whole balloon pushes outward, not just the spot you pressed. Pascal's Law says pressure in a closed liquid is passed on equally everywhere, which is why a light push can lift a heavy load.
