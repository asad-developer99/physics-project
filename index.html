Why 3D learning works

The transformative power of 3D models in physics

Strong theory isn't always enough — students need to see a concept before it clicks. Our simulations turn formulas into experiences students can manipulate and explore.

01

Visualize complex concepts with ease

Watch forces, energy and motion unfold in real time. Interactive 3D turns difficult topics into accessible, hands-on experiences that build genuine problem-solving confidence.

02

Boost retention and understanding

Visual learning strengthens memory and comprehension. Students move beyond memorizing formulas to understanding the science that produces them.

About this project

Built by an educator, for learners

All simulations on this site are created by E.P. Sajitha (Ph.D). Email me with feedback, corrections, or ideas for new simulations — new STEM modules are added at regular intervals.

The simulations are built with Three.js, an open-source, cross-browser JavaScript library and API for creating GPU-accelerated 3D graphics in the browser using WebGL.
