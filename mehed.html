About me

I'm Mehed Ali, a student who enjoys finding problems before anyone else does. In this project I test every simulation and present it to the audience, so that each formula, slider and 3D model works correctly and is easy to understand. I believe a good demo can explain physics better than a page of text.

After college, I want to work as a software tester or quality engineer and keep improving my presentation skills along the way.
