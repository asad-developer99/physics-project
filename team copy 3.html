About the project

These are interactive 3D physics simulations built with Three.js that run in the browser. They cover mechanics, optics, magnetism, electricity, thermodynamics and quantum topics. The team set out to make physics concepts easier to understand by letting students rotate, adjust and experiment with them, instead of only reading about them.
